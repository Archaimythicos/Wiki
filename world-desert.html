General Summations

The desert is made up of scattered tribes. These tribes aren’t that large, and there are no permanent coalitions. Truces and temporary alliances are sometimes made, but they never end very well. Everyone simply tries to survive in the vast sandy plains. There are disputes over water oases and hunting grounds, but mostly there is not much contact in day-to-day lives for the natives. They do not speak the vernacular, and there are many languages among tribes. There are translators, but the interpretation is very loose and not completely accurate. Most tribes are nomadic due to the constant sandstorms, moving with the herds of wild animals. They often walk throughout the entire night, stopping to rest only during the entire day when they set up camp. They are hunter-gatherers, sometimes settling in certain ideal locations for a few days before moving on. There are also raider groups which take advantage of the primitive nature of these tribes, ransacking camps and stealing valuables. Due to the aggressive activity of these raiders, even if tribes find ideal locations, they cannot settle for fear of attack.

Animals found in the desert are sparse, though herds of animals can sometimes be found wandering together for security. They have many traits adapted for the harsh environment but usually are not camouflaged well to avoid predators. Any weakness means they will likely become a predator’s meal the next day. Speed is crucial in the desert, to compete for limited resources. Certain species kill their young and the old, if they show signs of weakness. The strongest survive and often cannibalism is necessary for a group of individuals to survive. There are barely any plants found in the desert, though cacti are decently abundant in certain areas. The cacti can be the difference between life and death for any species, containing precious water inside its flesh.

Temperatures range anywhere between 30-35 degrees Celsius during the morning and Sunflare, spiking to around 38-40 degrees during Sunhigh. In the night, temperatures are lower than around 20-25 Celsius. However, occasional heat waves particularly during the hot season can cause temperatures to spike to highs of 45 Celsius. During the cold season, the temperature fluctuates greatly between day and night. In the evening, temperatures can sometimes dip below freezing, before rising again to around 25-30 Celsius come morning. Along with predators and the climate comes certain weather phenomena particular to the desert. Sandstorms are extremely difficult to deal with for the wandering desert tribes, and also for some of the animals. Winds can reach speeds of up to 50 km, sometimes going even higher. This can cause obvious issues like blowing debris and damaging structures like tribal teepees. The desert is an extremely harsh landscape, with barely any resources leaving species that survive hardened but primitive.

Architecture

Most of the desert tribes use animal hides combined with Adrastea roots found throughout the desert to construct tents that are lightweight and easy to take down and set up. The average family’s tent looks similar to a teepee, shaped like a cone. The chief’s family’s tent is larger and looks more like a circus tent. The tents all have a center pole and poles that lean on the center pole all made of Adrastea roots. The hides of the animals are then draped across the roots to finish the tent. The hides are made from majestic animals that the nomadic tribes happened to come across while hunting. Desert tribes that are larger and have permanent settlements construct small, one-story buildings to help withstand the deadly winds of the desert. These buildings are known as fallivors. They are constructed from condensed sand and stone that are extracted from the depths of the desert. Then, they smear a thick layer of mud and animal waste overtop to weatherproof the buildings. Fallivors only have one window and one door that can be quickly covered using animal hides to prevent wind and sand from blowing in. They are known to be sturdy and strong buildings that provide comfort and protection for the people. However, the poorer citizens sleep in the streets and in the small spaces between the fallivors to shield from the wind and sand. Settlements that are older and more advanced in technology have walls that are built around the group of fallivors in the middle.

Populations

The Desert has small nomadic tribes that are composed of around 20-30 people. These small tribes often travel around to follow their food source, and live in small teepees. In total, there are around 50 tribes who are actively living and hunting in the Desert. That puts the population of the Desert to be around 12,500 residents.
