General Summations

The Northern Isles are made up of many islands. These islands are rather small compared to the islands found in the south and center of the world. However, a strong, somewhat united confederation between the major islands makes up most of this region (red on the labeled map). This confederation, while powerful, has to deal with something, which prevents them from seeking war against other islands. The North contains many precious resources such as iron and gold. They are found in the two islands not part of the confederation. Conditions on these two islands are even harsher and more difficult to survive compared to the rest of the Isles. Not many have made settlements on the islands, but the existence of the precious resources found are known to the Confederation. The Confederation makes occasional journeys to mine the ores, but usually only in the warmer season. The existence of these two islands are actually not known to the rest of the world, as most who voyage so far north do not survive.

The Confederation is made up of many large towns and cities, with anything that one will need to live in the Northern Isles being available in these settlements. Those who live in the Northern Isles do not venture out much, due to the harsh winds and cold temperature. They mostly stay inside these cities and therefore isolate themselves from the rest of the world. The Confederation has methods of communication, usually by messengers. This communication is extremely slow, sometimes taking weeks to travel from one end of the alliance to the other. Thus, if the Confederation comes under attack, the rest of the islands are usually slow to respond. There is a single, massive city located in the heart of (insert name of the northernmost most central island). This serves as the main “capital” of the alliance. Additionally, the Isles’ isolation from the rest of the islands means that trade is not possible. They speak the vernacular, as the settlers who originally migrated to the Northern Isles come from the Warring Islands seeking refuge from the constant conflict. Most are relatively peaceful and are reluctant to wage war.

The cold weather obviously means that those who live in the Northern Isles must wear extremely heavy winter clothing (material and methods in clothing section of doc). They also cannot farm crops and standard produce due to the unsuitable climate. However, there are certain plants which can grow in the wintry environment. Certain animals such as mountain goats also live in the Isles, providing them with meat, milk, and other foods. Survival is difficult, so nothing can be wasted. Every animal killed has its entire body stripped and used for different purposes. No waste is tolerated, and nothing is meant to be enjoyed. Simply awakening for a new day is a blessing in itself. The temperature remains mostly the same throughout the year, never peaking above 5 degrees Celsius. The average is around minus 25 degrees- minus 15 degrees Celsius. The warm season lasts around 2-6 months, with temperatures increasing by around 5-10 degrees. There are only two seasons. Cold winds constantly sweep across the land (around 40 kmh.), creating almost a constant blizzard-like environment. There is usually around at least six inches of snow piled on the ground at any given time. Glacial formations and ice sheets can be found at the very north of these islands.

Architecture

The Northern Isles have an abundance of resources to aid them in advancing in technology. The inhabitants who choose to seek refuge there have formed into a civilization known as the Confederation where they take advantage of the abundance of wood and other resources and use them to develop architecturally advanced houses. Their major cities can have skyscrapers that can be 20-30 stories tall; how they manage to build such structures remain a mystery to the rest of the world. Along with skyscrapers, the Confederation has walls surrounding every town that are made from dense pine logs, stacked on top of one another until they form a 17 feet high protective shield from wild animals, enemies, and the bitter wind. Inside, the peasants live in one room houses that look like small round mushrooms. The roof can be opened and accessed as storage to help store frozen foods. The nobles live in larger houses that have separate rooms for sleeping, cooking, and lounging. All the houses in these small towns, are one story tall. Each town has a town hall, which is shaped like a barn, a triangular prism on top of a rectangular prism. In the larger cities, skyscrapers can hold up to 60 families. The tallest skyscrapers have around 30 stories and each story can hold up to two families. Large cities also have other buildings such as a fire station, police station, hospital, etc. They also have all the same buildings that the smaller towns have.

Populations

The Northern Isles population is unclear to the rest of the world. They are so isolated that people are rarely able to reach the Confederacy, and then there is getting back. However, using hints such as large amounts of footprints found scattered around the islands, and the massive amounts of disappearing trees, it can be inferred that the population of the Confederacy isn’t small and is a large, advanced civilization.
